Frequently Asked Questions



1. What is TravelDestination ?

TravelDestination is a premium, government-registered travel agency that provides fully managed local and international tour packages,
ticketing, and hotel reservations.


2. Is there a refund policy if I cancel my booking?

Yes, refunds are processed according to our cancellation policy. Cancellations made 15 days before the tour are eligible for a 70% refund,
while last-minute cancellations (within 48 hours) are non-refundable.


3. Are there any hidden charges in your tour prices?

No,TravelDestination believes in absolute transparency. All taxes, fuel surcharges, and service fees are included
in the final price shown to you.


4. What is included in a standard Travel Zone tour package?

A standard paid package typically includes return air/transport tickets, premium hotel accommodation, daily breakfast, a professional tour
guide, and site entry tickets.
